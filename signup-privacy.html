REGISTRATION PRIVACY

Know what happens to your information.

This registration summary explains the information relevant to creating an Apply4Company customer account. The full Privacy Policy remains the governing privacy document.

Effective: 08 September 2026 Last updated: 08 September 2026

1. Information You Provide

When registration and profile features are enabled, Apply4Company may handle information you provide, including your name, email address, career profile, resume information, skills, experience, education, job preferences, company preferences, and application-related information.

2. Why We Use It

Information may be used to create and maintain your account, provide company and opportunity discovery, support application workflows, track application activity, communicate service information, and maintain security and reliability.

3. External Application Platforms

When an application feature is enabled, information necessary for an authorized application may be transmitted to the supported external job platform. Naukri is the planned V1 platform, with additional platforms intended for future support.

External platforms may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Apply4Company should not request or retain an external platform password unless a future integration specifically requires it, is lawful, and is clearly disclosed to you.

4. Your Choices

Before production launch, the service will provide the applicable mechanisms for accessing, correcting, deleting, or otherwise managing personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

5. Browser Storage

The current login page can remember the email address you choose to save on the device using browser local storage. It does not store your password locally. Production documentation should be updated if this behavior changes.

Important: This is a registration-stage privacy summary, not a substitute for the full Privacy Policy or legal review. Final production notices must reflect the actual data flows, retention periods, service providers, cross-platform integrations, contact details, and applicable Indian data-protection requirements.

6. Full Privacy Policy

Read the complete Privacy Policy before creating your account.
