PRIVACY

Your information. Your trust.

This initial Privacy Policy explains the principles we intend to follow when handling information through Apply4Company.

Effective: 08 September 2026 Last updated: 08 September 2026

1. Overview

Apply4Company is a job-seeker-focused platform intended to help customers discover companies and opportunities, apply for roles through supported external application platforms, and organize their application journey.

This policy is a product-stage privacy document. It must be finalized against the production data flows, service providers, legal entity, retention schedule, grievance process, and applicable Indian data-protection requirements before production launch.

2. Information We May Handle

When the relevant features are enabled, Apply4Company may handle information such as:

  • Account and contact information, such as name and email address.
  • Professional profile information, including resume details, skills, experience, education, and career preferences.
  • Company and opportunity preferences supplied by the customer.
  • Application information, application status, dates, and external platform identifiers where applicable.
  • Technical, device, and security information required to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

We should collect and use only information that is necessary for the stated service purposes and should not describe a category as collected unless it is actually used by the production product.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain customer accounts.
  • To provide company and opportunity discovery.
  • To use customer-provided information to support authorized application workflows.
  • To track and organize application activity and progress.
  • To communicate important service, account, security, and support information.
  • To maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and protect the security of the service.

4. Applications and External Platforms

When a customer authorizes an application workflow, Apply4Company may transmit the information necessary to submit or support the application through the supported external job platform. Naukri is planned as the V1 application platform; the architecture is intended to support additional platforms in future versions.

External platforms and employers may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Apply4Company should not request or retain an external platform password unless a future integration specifically requires it, is lawful, and is clearly disclosed to the customer.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers or external systems when necessary to provide an enabled feature, operate and secure the service, comply with a legal obligation, or carry out an action authorized by the customer.

Before production launch, the final privacy notice should identify material categories of processors/service providers and the relevant data flows where required by applicable law.

6. Security

Apply4Company intends to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards for customer information. Production authentication, access control, encryption, secrets management, audit logging, backups, and sensitive-data handling must be implemented through secure backend infrastructure rather than relying on client-side controls.

7. Password Recovery and Account Security

For password recovery, the service may process your account email, a short-lived one-time verification code, reset-session metadata, and security/audit information needed to protect the recovery process. Passwords must not be stored in plain text, and the browser must not store passwords or OTPs in local storage or session storage.

The production account service should enforce one-time use and expiry for recovery codes, rate-limit recovery attempts, limit OTP verification attempts, protect recovery sessions, and prevent a new password from matching the user's previous 3 passwords. Password history should be stored only as secure password hashes and access to it should be restricted.

After a successful password reset, the account service should revoke active sessions or refresh tokens as appropriate. Recovery emails and security logs should avoid exposing passwords, OTP values, or reset tokens.

8. Retention, Deletion, and Customer Requests

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, and operational requirements. Production retention periods and deletion schedules must be documented before launch.

Where applicable, customers should have clear mechanisms to request access, correction, deletion, or other available controls over their personal information, subject to applicable law.

9. Browser Storage and Similar Technologies

The current login page can store the email address selected through “Remember me” in browser local storage. The current frontend does not store the password locally. If cookies, analytics, advertising technologies, or other tracking mechanisms are introduced, the production privacy documentation should explain what is used, why it is used, and what choices are available.

10. Children's Information

The service is intended for people who are legally able to use the service under applicable requirements. If the product is made available to children or processes children's personal data, the production privacy, consent, age-assurance, and safety controls must be updated accordingly.

11. Grievances and Privacy Contact

For privacy or service concerns, use the Contact page. If Apply4Company is subject to intermediary-specific grievance obligations, the required Grievance Officer name, contact details, and grievance mechanism must be prominently published before production launch.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product and its data practices evolve. Material changes should be reflected by updating the effective or last-updated date and providing any notice required by applicable law.

Important: This page is not a legal certification. Final production compliance must be reviewed against the applicable Indian laws and rules, including the Digital Personal Data Protection framework and, if applicable to the service's legal classification, the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 as amended.
